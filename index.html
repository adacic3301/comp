Where you'll be

Berchtesgaden, between the Watzmann and the Königssee

Both flats sit inside the Berchtesgadener Land, a stretch of Bavaria where the mountains start at the edge of town rather than an hour's drive away. Trailheads, alpine lakes and mountain huts are close enough to reach on foot or with a short drive.

Salzburg is roughly 30 minutes away by car if you want a city day, but most guests find they don't need to leave the valley at all.
