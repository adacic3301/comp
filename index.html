Where you'll be

Berchtesgaden, between the Watzmann and the Königssee

Both flats sit inside the Berchtesgadener Land, a stretch of Bavaria where the mountains start at the edge of town rather than an hour's drive away. Trailheads, alpine lakes and mountain huts are close enough to reach on foot or with a short drive.

Salzburg is roughly 30 minutes away by car if you want a city day, but most guests find they don't need to leave the valley at all.

The whole area sits inside Germany's only alpine national park, so the view from the window tends to be the same one you'll be hiking into that afternoon.

Sights & day trips

What's actually worth your time nearby

A short, honest list: not everything within an hour's drive, just the places guests keep coming back and telling us about.

~15 min drive

Königssee

A fjord-like alpine lake with electric boats. The crossing to the onion-domed chapel at St. Bartholomä is the classic first trip.

~20 min drive + shuttle bus

Kehlsteinhaus (Eagle's Nest)

A historic building on a mountain ridge with a panoramic terrace over the whole valley. Open seasonally, roughly May to October.

From the doorstep

Watzmann hiking trails

Everything from flat valley walks to the multi-day Watzmann traverse starts within the national park, minutes from both flats.

~15 min drive

Rossfeld panoramic road

Germany's highest through-road, tracing the Austrian border with pull-offs for wide views of the Watzmann and Hoher Göll.

~10 min drive

Berchtesgaden salt mine

An underground tour through centuries-old salt tunnels, including a slide and a short boat crossing. A solid rainy-day option, good for most ages.

~10 min drive

Obersalzberg Documentation Centre

A museum covering the region's 20th-century history in detail, with remnants of wartime tunnel systems open to visit beneath it.

Good to know

Planning the practical side of the trip

Getting here

Salzburg Airport is about 45 minutes away, Munich Airport around two hours by car. Berchtesgaden also has its own train station with regular regional connections.

Guest card & buses

Overnight guests in the Berchtesgadener Land typically receive a regional guest card, which covers local bus travel. Handy for reaching Königssee or the valley trailheads without a car.

Parking & arrival

Private parking is available at both apartments. We'll send exact check-in instructions and directions a few days before arrival.

When to visit

Summer suits hiking and lake days; winter brings skiing at the Jenner and Rossfeld. Spring and autumn are quieter, with the trails mostly to yourself.
